The recipe fixes the cost structure.

The listed ingredient weights total exactly 250 g before the spice sachet. This study therefore treats the spice sachet as an additional packed component outside the 250 g food fill. If the legal net content must remain exactly 250 g including spices, reduce the grain component by the sachet weight.

Base SKU

Chicken + rice

Use this as the process-validation SKU because Tamil Nadu offers strong poultry availability and rice avoids importing wheat from a distant producing region.

Second SKU

Buffalo-beef + rice

Use only approved, traceable, FSSAI-compliant buffalo-meat suppliers and validate export-market labeling and religious certification requirements.

Variant strategy

Pair vegetables by availability

Corn/scallion and peas/green pepper should be treated as separate formulations because fresh price, dehydration yield and sensory performance differ materially.

Critical formulation issue: 11 g of dried mushroom can require roughly 100–110 g fresh mushroom depending on variety and final moisture. At current Madurai/Tuticorin market prices, mushroom becomes one of the largest raw-material costs per pack. Validate the sensory requirement before locking the recipe.
03 / Location recommendation

Recommend Dindigul.
Benchmark Tuticorin.

01

Input proximity matters more than port adjacency

The plant processes fresh vegetables and fresh/frozen protein but exports a much lighter, shelf-stable finished product. Moving finished cartons 180–220 km to port is normally cheaper than hauling high-moisture ingredients to a port-side factory every day.

02

Dindigul sits inside an agricultural catchment

Tamil Nadu agriculture data records paddy, maize and horticulture in Dindigul district; official horticulture references also identify Dindigul as a carrot-growing district. Nearby Theni has a dedicated food-park strategy built around paddy, maize and horticulture.

03

Poultry sourcing is stronger inland

Dindigul/Palani is within the South Tamil Nadu poultry network and closer to the Namakkal–Erode–Karur production belt than Tuticorin.

04

Tuticorin remains the export gateway

VOC Port gives the project an established container port. Current public freight listings show Tuticorin → Apapa service options, while Colombo connectivity gives additional West Africa routing choices.

07 / Technical operating model

Separate raw zones.
Converge after kill steps.

Receive & inspect→Cold / dry storage→Veg wash & cut→Meat cook / debone→Grain cook / instantize→Dehydrate→Cool & condition→Blend→250 g fill→Spice sachet→Metal detect→Carton & export
Rated base line
1.0 t/day
finished dry food on one main shift
Pack output
4,000/day
250 g packs; ~104,000/month at 26 days
Hygiene design
3 zones
raw protein · raw vegetables · post-kill high-care

Chicken / meat stream

Receive chilled/frozen approved meat → temper under control → cook/boil → debone/size reduce → dehydrate → verify final moisture/water activity → high-care transfer. Capture cooking liquor separately for future broth recovery.

Rice / wheat stream

Clean → wash where applicable → controlled cook/steam or extrusion/instantization → dehydrate → condition. Because grain dominates the formula, line selection should be based on rehydration time and eating quality, not only throughput.

Vegetable stream

Wash → inspect → cut → product-specific blanch/pretreat → dewater → low-temperature staged dehydration → cool → grade. Mushroom should be validated independently because yield and browning control are critical.

Packing stream

Recipe-controlled blending → checkweigh → 250 g pouch fill → insert spice sachet → seal → metal detect → code → carton → palletize. Nitrogen flush or high-barrier laminate may be required after shelf-life validation.

13 / UAE free-zone question

Useful for trading.
Not required for shipping.

01

India entity only

Best starting structure if the Indian company owns the factory, contracts ECOWAS buyers and exports directly. Lowest administration and no unnecessary UAE layer.

02

Add UAE later

Useful if buyers prefer UAE contracts/banking, you want centralized regional trading, GCC sales, investor participation, multi-country procurement or re-export warehousing.

03

Avoid physical re-export without reason

Routing India cargo through Jebel Ali before West Africa adds handling, time and freight unless you are consolidating, relabeling, financing or serving UAE/GCC customers.

QuestionAnswer
Can India ship directly to ECOWAS?Yes. Current carrier networks serve West Africa directly from western India and via transshipment options from southern India.
Does a UAE free-zone company reduce Indian export licensing?No. The Indian manufacturer/exporter still needs its Indian compliance and export documentation.
Can a UAE free-zone company re-export?Yes. UAE official guidance confirms free-zone businesses can export/re-export internationally and goods kept in the free zone for re-export generally do not attract UAE mainland customs duty.
Recommended kickoff structureIndian operating/export company first. Add UAE only when a commercial need is proven.

16 / Product 2 — jelly broth

Turn cooking liquor into a Phase-2 revenue stream.

Do not include broth revenue in the kickoff investment case. Capture and measure it from day one, then commercialize 6–9 months after the instant-food line reaches stable capacity and the broth process has validated shelf life.

Meat cooking liquor→Coarse filtration→Fat separation→Fine filtration→Vacuum concentration→Season / standardize→Gel set / fill→Retort or validated preservation

Food-safety warning: a high-moisture meat broth gel is microbiologically sensitive. If the intended product is ambient-stable, a validated thermal process/retort or equivalent preservation system is required. A refrigerated gel creates a cold-chain burden for ECOWAS. Compare ambient gel, retort pouch, concentrated paste and powder before final product selection.

18 / Implementation roadmap

Release capital against evidence.

01

Recipe & yield trials

Produce chicken-rice, buffalo-rice and one wheat variant at pilot scale. Measure all fresh/raw inputs, trim loss, cook loss, drying recovery, energy, final moisture, aw and rehydration.

02

Supplier map

Secure 3–5 suppliers for rice/wheat, poultry/buffalo meat, mushroom and each vegetable. Obtain seasonal volume/price schedules and transport costs.

03

Buyer validation

Send samples to importers/distributors in at least Nigeria and Ghana plus one Francophone ECOWAS market. Obtain target landed prices, pack format, registration and shelf-life requirements.

04

Site shortlist

Visit Dindigul/Vedachandur, Madurai north and one Tuticorin benchmark property. Verify power load, water, effluent route, fire access, food use and truck movement.

05

Vendor tender

Issue one common URS to at least three line vendors so dryer, grain, protein and packing quotations are technically comparable.

06

Licensing + fit-out

Incorporate/export setup, FoSCoS classification, pollution/fire/factory approvals, HACCP plan, floor zoning and utility installation.

07

Commission & validate

IQ/OQ/PQ-style acceptance, microbiology, shelf-life, distribution simulation, metal detector challenge, coding and traceability tests.

08

Broth Phase 2 gate

After 6–9 months at stable capacity, review measured broth volume/composition and choose gel, retort paste or powder based on market and shelf-life evidence.

Investment committee direction

Proceed to pilot + Dindigul site due diligence.

Do not commit the full ₹5.2–8.6 crore fixed setup until the 11 g mushroom inclusion, protein dry yield, instant-grain performance and buyer wholesale price are proven with physical trials and written commercial feedback.
